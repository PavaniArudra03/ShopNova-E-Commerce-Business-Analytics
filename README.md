# ShopNova E-Commerce Business Analytics

## Overview

ShopNova E-Commerce Business Analytics is an end-to-end data analytics project focused on analyzing e-commerce business performance across sales, customers, products, marketing campaigns, and returns.

The project uses MySQL, Excel, Tableau, and Power BI to transform raw business data into meaningful insights, interactive dashboards, and actionable business recommendations.

The analysis covers the period from January to December 2023 and provides a comprehensive view of revenue performance, customer behavior, product performance, marketing effectiveness, and return patterns.

## Objectives

* Analyze overall sales and revenue performance
* Track key business performance indicators
* Understand customer purchasing behavior and loyalty
* Identify high-performing products, brands, and categories
* Analyze sales performance across different channels
* Evaluate marketing campaign performance and return on investment
* Analyze product returns, refund patterns, and customer feedback
* Identify business trends, opportunities, and areas for improvement
* Build interactive dashboards for business reporting and decision-making

## Key Business Insights

### Sales and Revenue

* The business generated approximately ₹140.37 million in revenue across 7,562 orders.
* Average Order Value was approximately ₹18,563.
* Electronics was the dominant revenue-generating category with approximately ₹108.19 million in revenue.
* Revenue showed a noticeable increase during October and November.

### Customer Analysis

* Repeat customers generated a significant portion of overall orders.
* There were 4,428 repeat orders compared with 3,134 first-time orders.
* The Mobile App recorded 3,843 orders, compared with 2,778 orders through the Website.
* The VIP customer segment represented high-value customers.
* Several top customers generated lifetime sales in the range of approximately ₹6–8 lakh.

### Product Analysis

* Electronics contributed approximately 77% of total revenue.
* Apple, Sony, and Samsung were among the leading brands.
* Apple iPhone 14 128GB and Sony Alpha ZV-E10L were among the standout products.
* The product portfolio contained 87 SKUs across six categories.

### Returns Analysis

* There were 574 returns during the analysis period.
* The overall return rate was approximately 7.59%.
* "Changed Mind" was identified as a leading return reason.
* Approximately 81% of returns were processed or refunded.
* Return resolution time extended up to seven days.
* Neutral and dissatisfied customer feedback represented an important area for improvement.

### Marketing Analysis

* The dataset contained 108 marketing campaigns.
* Campaign analysis was used to evaluate spend, conversions, revenue, and ROAS.
* Marketing performance was analyzed across channels and customer segments.
* Geographic and customer-level analysis helped identify stronger-performing markets and segments.

## Business Recommendations

Based on the analysis, the following recommendations were identified:

### 1. Diversify Category Performance

Reduce dependency on Electronics by increasing focus on high-potential categories such as Fashion and Home & Kitchen.

### 2. Strengthen Customer Loyalty

Develop loyalty programs and VIP customer initiatives to increase repeat purchases and customer lifetime value.

### 3. Reduce Avoidable Returns

Analyze product-level return patterns and improve product information, specifications, images, and customer guidance to reduce avoidable returns.

### 4. Optimize Marketing Spend

Allocate marketing budgets toward channels, campaigns, and customer segments that demonstrate stronger ROAS and acquisition performance.

### 5. Improve Return Experience

Monitor return resolution time and customer feedback to improve the overall post-purchase experience.
